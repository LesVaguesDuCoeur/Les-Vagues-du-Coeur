The History of Tea

Tea is one of the most popular beverages in the world. Its history begins in ancient China over 5,000 years ago. According to legend, Emperor Shen Nong discovered tea when leaves from a wild tree blew into his pot of boiling water. Today, there are many different types of tea, including green, black, and oolong, all coming from the same plant: Camellia sinensis. The differences in flavor are due to how the leaves are processed.
